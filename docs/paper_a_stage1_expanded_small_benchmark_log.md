## 1. Goal

Stage 1 evaluates the latest RG-RALNS implementation on a slightly broader seed set to determine whether the seed 2 service degradation observed in Stage 0 and Stage 0.5 is isolated or part of a recurring instability pattern. This is a benchmark diagnosis task only: no new baselines were added, no final-scale experiments were run, and neither the online visibility protocol nor the normalized objective definition was changed.

## 12. Stability Interpretation

Answers to Stage 1 stability questions:

1. Seed 2 is not the only severe WSF/ZSR outlier. Seed 4 is also severe for RG-RALNS with `WSF=3.20` and `ZSR=0.667`.
2. Seed 3 does not show the same failure; RG-RALNS reaches `WSF=0` and `ZSR=1.0`.
3. RG-RALNS has better mean WSF/ZSR than Online-Legacy-ALNS, but the seed-level picture is mixed.
4. RG-RALNS is not uniformly at least as good as Lightweight RG Dispatch: seed 4 is worse in WSF and Z_N.

## 14. Mechanism Interpretation

Trigger ratios are not close to 1.0:

```text
seed 0: 0.255
seed 1: 0.136
seed 2: 0.614
seed 3: 0.237
seed 4: 0.337
```

The affected set remains local:

```text
avg_A_size ranges from 3.32 to 7.07
max_A_size <= 8
```

The main warning sign is fallback/no-ready behavior:

```text
seed 2: no_ready_operation_available = 29
seed 4: no_ready_operation_available = 7
```

Seed 2 is still the strongest no-ready/fallback stress case. Seed 4 has fewer fallback failures but a larger final WSF, so it may involve an earlier prioritization/capacity allocation issue rather than only no-ready fallback.

## 15. Stage 2 Readiness Decision

RG-RALNS is not yet ready for Stage 2 main benchmark as a finalized algorithm.

Reason:

```text
The mean results are strong, but seed-level robustness is not yet sufficient.
Seed 2 and seed 4 both show service degradation.
Seed 4 also shows that RG-RALNS can lose to Lightweight RG Dispatch on WSF/Z_N for an individual seed.
```

This corresponds to Case C with an additional seed-level Case D warning:

```text
multiple seeds show WSF/ZSR failures;
the next fix should target capacity-aware local decode or rescue-chain machine reservation.
```

## 16. Next Recommended Task

Before Stage 2, run a Stage 1.5 stabilization round focused on:

```text
1. seed 4 rescue failure trace;
2. capacity-aware local decode over A(t);
3. service-rescue machine reservation or bottleneck protection;
4. acceptance guard that detects worsening final service feasibility within visible local context;
5. ablation: RG-RALNS vs Lightweight RG on seeds 2 and 4 only.
```

The goal should be to preserve the strong mean behavior while reducing seed-level WSF outliers.
